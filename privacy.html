Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains how our Discord moderation bot handles information.

2. Information Collection

The bot is intended not to store user information. However, it may temporarily process Discord user IDs, server IDs, messages, or moderation-related information to perform its functions.

3. Use of Information

Information may be processed as necessary to provide moderation features, respond to commands, maintain security, and troubleshoot problems.

4. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Discord and hosting providers may process information according to their own policies.

5. Data Retention

We aim to avoid retaining user information unnecessarily. Actual retention depends on the bot's configuration and hosting environment.

6. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect information, but absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the bot owner through the appropriate Discord support channel.
